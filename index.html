Drs'. Drip Lounge & Infusion Bar

A lounge built only for IV drips.

Drs'. Drip offers full-service IV drip therapy for all ages. The idea is simple: a doctor checks you first, you pick the drip that fits your goal, and you relax in a quiet chair while it runs.

The lounge also has skin and body treatment machines, so you can pair your drip with a treatment in the same visit.

The Drs'. Drip method

Check first. Drip second.

Every session starts with a doctor's screening, not a menu. Your health comes before any drip.

  1. 01

    Screen

    A doctor reviews your history, allergies, medicines and vital signs.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Together you pick the drip that fits your goal and your body.

  3. 03

    Relax

    Settle into a lounge chair while trained staff run and watch your drip.

  4. 04

    Go

    Get aftercare tips and plan your next visit if you need one.

Safety, always

Doctor-led. Never DIY.

IV drips are medical procedures, so they belong in a clinic, not at home. At Drs'. Drip, every drip is:

  • ScreenedA doctor checks you before your first drip, and again whenever your health changes.
  • SterileSingle-use kits, prepared in a clean infusion bar.
  • WatchedTrained staff stay nearby for your whole session.
  • HonestIf a drip isn't right for you, we'll tell you.
