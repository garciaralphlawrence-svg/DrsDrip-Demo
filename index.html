Drs'. Drip Lounge & Infusion Bar opened as the country's first IV drip lounge. Thirteen years later, we're a health, wellness and aesthetic center with three branches and a team you've trusted for over a decade.

Every service starts with a medical assessment. Nothing is done without a physician's approval.
